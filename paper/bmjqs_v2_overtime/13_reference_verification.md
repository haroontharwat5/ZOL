# Reference verification report

Verified 2 May 2026 by web search of PubMed, journal pages, and the issuing body for HSSIB. Each reference was checked for (a) bibliographic correctness and (b) whether the specific claim made in the paper draft matches what the source actually reports.

Status legend:
- **CLEAN** — bibliographic details correct and claim matches source.
- **AUTHOR/CITATION ERROR** — paper exists but cited authors, journal, volume, or pages are wrong.
- **CLAIM MISMATCH** — paper exists but our paper attributes a number, finding, or framing that the source does not actually contain.
- **CLAIM OVERSTATED** — paper exists but the wording in our paper goes beyond what the source supports.

## Patient-harm and after-hours mortality

### Ref 6 — Cortegiani A et al. *Br J Anaesth* 2020;124(5):623-37 — **CLEAN**
- PMID 32147102. DOI 10.1016/j.bja.2020.01.019.
- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32147102/
- Quote from paper: *"the primary analysis from adjusted estimates demonstrated an association between night/after-hours surgery and a higher risk of mortality (odds ratio [OR]=1.16; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.06-1.28; P=0.002; number of studies=18; I2=67%) based on low certainty evidence."*
- Our claim "adjusted odds ratio of 1.16 (95% CI 1.06 to 1.28)" matches.
- **Caveat to add to the draft:** the meta-analysis labels the underlying evidence as low-certainty (GRADE). A cautious one-sentence acknowledgement would strengthen the claim.

### Ref 7 — Oh TK, Song IA. *Anaesthesia* 2025 — **AUTHOR/CITATION ERROR**
- DOI 10.1111/anae.16559.
- Link: https://associationofanaesthetists-publications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/anae.16559
- The paper has TWO authors: Oh TK and Song IA. "Jo Y-Y" should be removed from our reference list.
- Quote from paper: *"after a 1:5 propensity score matching, 281,717 were included (57,497 in the after-hours group and 224,220 in the in-hours group)."*
- Our claim "281,717-patient propensity-matched cohort from South Korea" matches.
- **Important contextual issue:** The paper reports OR 3.58 for 90-day mortality, much larger than Cortegiani's pooled OR 1.16. Sakurai (Ref 26) attributes this to inadequate confounder control given a 4-fold imbalance in emergency procedures even after matching. The draft should not present Oh 2025 as a clean confirmation — pair it with the critique.

### Ref 8 — Saager L et al. *Anesthesiology* 2014;121(4):695-706 — **CLEAN**
- PMID 25102312.
- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25102312/
- Quote: *"incidence of 8.8, 11.6, 14.2, 17.0, and 21.2% for patients with 0, 1, 2, 3, and ≥4 transitions; odds ratio 1.08 [95% CI, 1.05 to 1.10] for an increase of 1 transition category."*

- **Caveat:** A follow-up Cleveland Clinic study (Terekhov et al. 2016, PMID 27466034) reached the opposite conclusion. We can keep Saager as the primary citation but the discussion of handover risk should not read as settled.
