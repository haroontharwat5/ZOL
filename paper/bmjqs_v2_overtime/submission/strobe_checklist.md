# STROBE Statement — checklist for cross-sectional / observational studies

**Manuscript:** Where does operating-room overtime come from? A retrospective single-centre study

**Design:** Retrospective observational (cross-sectional) study of administrative operating-room data.

This checklist maps each STROBE item to the section of the manuscript where it is addressed. Page numbers should be completed against the final typeset PDF before submission.
