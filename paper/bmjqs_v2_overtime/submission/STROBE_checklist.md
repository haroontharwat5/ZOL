# STROBE Statement — Checklist for cross-sectional/observational studies

Reporting checklist for the manuscript *"Where operating-room overtime concentrates and why: a retrospective single-centre study."* Page and section references point to the submitted manuscript. This is a retrospective observational (cross-sectional analysis of routinely collected administrative data) study; items specific to cohort follow-up or case-control sampling are marked not applicable (N/A).
