Three Findings That Challenge the Textbook
1. FCOTS Does Not Predict Stratified Overtime
Genk has the worst first-case punctuality (25.1 min net delay) and the worst overtime (8.4%). But this correlation is spurious — both are driven by operating-model complexity. The Cathlab has similar FCOTS to Maaseik but double the overtime rate.
2. Duration Estimation Is Not the Problem
CVs within planned-duration buckets are comparable across all four sites. The scheduling teams share the same EHR and historical data. Forecasting accuracy does not explain the between-site performance gaps.
3. Cascading Delays Waste 4x More Time Than Overruns
The dominant source of wasted OR time is not surgeries running long — it is delays stacking up through the day. A 10-minute first-case delay can snowball into 60 minutes by end of day. Process discipline is a larger lever than prediction.
